Talli Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

Talli ("Talli," "we," "us," or "our") provides a shared-expense tracking application (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we process when you use the Service, why we process it, and the choices available to you.

For privacy questions, contact us at support@talli.hamfri.me.

Information we collect

Information you provide

We collect the information needed to create and use your account, including your name or display name, email address, and password. Passwords are stored as hashes, not in readable form.

We also process content that you add to the Service, including:

  • group names, currencies, and member names or email addresses;
  • expenses, payment allocations, settlement records, notes, dates, and amounts;
  • group invitation details and password-reset requests; and
  • receipt images or PDFs that you choose to upload, together with their file name, type, size, checksum, and extracted receipt details.

If you use Google Sign-In or Sign in with Apple, we receive an identity token from that provider and use it to authenticate or link your Talli account. We do not receive or store your Google or Apple password.

Information generated through use of the Service

We create records needed to operate and protect the Service, such as account identifiers, authentication-session information, timestamps, group roles, resource versions, and an activity history showing changes to shared groups, expenses, settlements, and receipts. The activity history may include the name or identifier of the member who made a change and relevant before-and-after ledger details.

We may also process limited technical information that is routinely sent when the Service communicates with our servers, such as request time, device or browser information, IP address, and error or security logs. We use this information for security, troubleshooting, and service operation.

How we use information

We use personal information to:

  • provide the Service, including maintaining shared groups, calculating balances, and showing settlement suggestions;
  • authenticate users, maintain sessions, prevent fraud and abuse, and protect the Service;
  • send group invitations and password-reset emails that you request or that a group administrator initiates;
  • process receipt uploads and, when enabled, extract receipt details to help populate an itemized expense;
  • maintain an activity history for group transparency and resolve support or technical issues; and
  • comply with applicable law and enforce our terms.

We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or use it to make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

How information is shared

Talli is designed for shared expenses. Information in a group—including member names, expense and settlement details, balances, and relevant activity history—is visible to the other members of that group, subject to their group role and the Service’s access controls. Only add people to a group when you are comfortable sharing that information with them.

We use service providers that process information for us to operate the Service:

  • Hosting, database, and private object storage providers host application data and uploaded receipts.
  • Google verifies Google Sign-In identity tokens. If receipt extraction is enabled, Talli sends the receipt file you chose to upload to Google Gemini for automated document-data extraction.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple authentication when you choose that option.
  • Resend sends group-invitation and password-reset emails and may process delivery, bounce, complaint, and suppression events.

These providers process information to provide their services, subject to their applicable terms and privacy commitments. We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, investigate misuse, or in connection with a corporate transaction, subject to applicable law.

Receipt uploads and extraction

Receipt upload is optional. The app may request camera access only when you choose to scan a receipt; you can instead choose a supported file. Uploaded receipts are stored in private object storage and are accessed through time-limited links. Before each receipt upload for automated extraction, the app asks for your permission to share that receipt with Google Gemini and offers manual itemization instead. If you continue, the receipt bytes are sent to Google Gemini so it can return structured fields such as merchant name, date, totals, line items, and adjustments. Declining leaves you able to enter the expense manually without sending that receipt to Gemini.

Pending-upload, uploaded, and failed receipt drafts are eligible for cleanup after their configured expiry, currently 24 hours. Successfully extracted drafts that have not been attached may remain longer. A receipt attached to an expense is retained with that expense until it is removed, replaced, or deleted through the Service, including deletion of the uploading account. We request file deletion after the relevant change is committed, and scheduled cleanup retries eligible deletions. Storage failures can delay removal; deletion does not guarantee immediate erasure of every copy. Associated ledger or activity records may remain for group history, security, or legal obligations.

Data retention

We retain account, group, and ledger information for as long as your account or the relevant group is active, and afterward as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including maintaining the shared ledger, security, dispute resolution, and legal compliance. Some records, including audit history and records related to deleted ledger entries, may be retained to preserve group history and the integrity of the ledger.

Authentication refresh sessions currently expire after 30 days and expired session records are removed on a scheduled basis. Password-reset links are single-use and currently expire after 30 minutes.

Your choices and rights

You can review and update many group and expense details through the Service. You may sign out at any time; signing out removes the stored refresh token from the device. You can control camera permission in your device settings.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, deletion of, restriction of, or portability of your personal information, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, contact us at support@talli.hamfri.me. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. Some information may need to be retained, for example to maintain the shared ledger, meet legal obligations, or protect the Service.

You can delete your account from Profile in the app. Account deletion is irreversible. Before deletion, you must transfer any active groups you own and settle any balances in active groups. Once deletion succeeds, we remove sign-in credentials and sessions, clear the account email and display name, and archive active memberships with a deleted-user label. Shared expenses, settlements, internal identifiers, and historical activity records remain for other group members. Those records, including previous values and user-entered notes, may still contain identifying information; account deletion does not anonymize all historical content. We also request deletion of receipts uploaded by the account, subject to the cleanup delays described above. Contact us about personal information remaining in shared records.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information. For example, the mobile app stores its refresh token in platform secure storage on supported native devices, uses rotating authentication tokens, and uses time-limited links for receipt transfers. No security measure is perfect, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

International transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than the one where you live, including where our service providers operate. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

Children

Talli is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age required by applicable law to consent to online services. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us at support@talli.hamfri.me.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version and change the effective date. If a change is material, we will provide additional notice when required by law.
